🎸 A tuner, a metronome, a chord detector and automatic tab scrolling - all by voice, without ever letting go of your guitar or piano: the musician's Swiss Army knife in your browser, compatible with Ultimate Guitar and YouTube.

🗣️ VOICE COMMANDS
Say your assistant's name ("Roddy" by default) followed by a command. Microphone access is required.

📺 HANDS-FREE YOUTUBE TUTORIALS
- Play / pause: "Roddy, play", "Roddy, pause".
- Smart cues: drop cues at key moments and jump between them by voice. "Roddy, Encore" snaps back to the last cue to loop a riff.

📜 AUTOMATIC TAB SCROLLING (ULTIMATE GUITAR, ETC.)
- Start scrolling: "Roddy, c'est parti!".
- Set the tempo: "Roddy, plus vite" / "moins vite", or target "Roddy, vitesse 4".

🔍 VOICE SEARCH (GOOGLE, YOUTUBE, ULTIMATE GUITAR)
- "Roddy, cherche [song]" runs the search.
- "Roddy, ouvre 3" opens the numbered result.
- "Roddy, cherche [artist] sur YouTube" targets the platform.

🛠️ BUILT-IN TOOLS
- Precision tuner
- Audio and visual metronome
- Real-time chord detector
- Pitch chart (ideal for singing)
- Repertoire drawer: transpositions, capos, and per-song notes

🔒 PRIVACY
Voice processing runs in your browser. No audio recording is sent to the extension creator or any third-party server.
